How it works

From upload to a clinician-ready report in four steps.

1

Upload

Securely upload a mammogram, chest X-ray, CT or histopathology image.

2

Pre-process

Normalization, resizing and quality checks prepare the image for the model.

3

Analyze

A convolutional neural network scores risk and flags regions of interest.

4

Report

Confidence score and heatmap are returned for clinician review.

AI models

Different architectures suit different imaging types. All use transfer learning from ImageNet.

ResNet-50

Reliable all-rounder for mammography, MRI and CT classification. Dataset ideas: CBIS-DDSM, BraTS.

DenseNet-121

Popular for chest X-rays (the CheXNet family). Dataset ideas: NIH ChestX-ray14, LUNA16.

EfficientNet-B4

High accuracy at low cost for skin and pathology images. Dataset ideas: ISIC, LC25000.

U-Net

Segmentation model that outlines the exact tumor boundary, useful for brain MRI and liver CT.

Vision Transformer

Captures global context in large histopathology slides. Dataset idea: Camelyon.

Grad-CAM

Explainability layer that produces the heatmap showing where the model looked.
